# Virtually Enhanced Senses (VES)

**An open research platform for sensory augmentation, human-machine interaction, and action-perception research.**

Virtually Enhanced Senses (VES) is a modular, distributed platform designed to support the development and study of sensorimotor interactions in real, virtual, and mixed environments.

VES integrates heterogeneous hardware and software components — including embedded devices, sensors, actuators, motion-tracking systems, and processing services — through a common architecture for discovery, configuration, orchestration, and real-time data exchange.

Its goal is to provide a flexible and reusable technological foundation for building, operating, and studying experimental systems involving multimodal sensory feedback and human-machine interaction.
